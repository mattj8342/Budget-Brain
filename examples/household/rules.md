# Fictional household rules

All identifiers, amounts and scenarios in this example are fabricated.

## Funding order

Cover eligible overspending, verified monthly bills and annual bills due before the next payday. Review card debt/reserve gaps, then fund Essentials, the required buffer, and selected remaining targets. Work expenses are excluded from automatic household funding.

## Weekly behavior

This example household chooses two-week payday coverage. Weekly refill targets reach two weekly amounts in Available, counting carryover. Weekly set-aside targets receive two contributions, subtracting reviewed contributions already made for that actual cycle. Do not infer that amount from monthly net Assigned.

Other cadences use native remaining funding. Across month boundaries review coverage through the next payday. A midcycle catch-up never creates another full allowance.

## Cards and goals

The example reserve floor is $25, capped at current debt. Fully reserved cards receive no addition. Discuss gaps before choosing the reserve or supported extra contributions. Category assignments are not bank payments.

Live YNAB supplies target amount, mode and full recurrence. A non-recurring spending goal counts funding already spent in its goal period. A Have-a-balance goal requires unspent Available. Clothing repeats every three months in the synthetic snapshot.

## Approval

Resolve material planning questions, prepare the exact server preview, and obtain one explicit final confirmation that both partners agree. A changed payload, identity, settings revision, budget state or expiry requires renewed review. A demo result is never approval for a live write.

Existing members reuse shared household settings. Drafts and historical records do not authorize replay.
